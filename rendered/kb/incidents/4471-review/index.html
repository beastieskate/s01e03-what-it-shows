#4471 review · two causes, different outcomes

owner support deskclosedreviewed 2026-11-03

Since April, people have reported bookings landing on the wrong day. The desk merged 412 unique reports into #4471; the export contained 15 duplicate rows.

The reports describe two separate problems. Both affected tenants whose locale writes the day before the month, but they entered through different date-reading paths.

Engineering outcome

The course's live engineering update marks #4471 closed on 5 October 2026. The two problems have different closure reasons:

  • Parser — fixed. Engineering shipped fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Issue #1 is closed as completed.
  • Hint — won't fix. The booking-v3 form was retired, not repaired. Issue #2 is closed as not planned.

The counts below describe the historical export; closing the parent ticket does not mean that both problems were fixed.

Problems

Shared parser reads day-first dates as month-first — 379 reports

Before the fix, the shared parser read every date month first. For day-first tenants, an input such as 07/04/2026 was interpreted as July 4 instead of April 7. This is the larger cause group and includes reports from app, email, and form channels.

GitHub ticket: parser: day-first dates are read month-first

Booking-v3 hint disagrees with the tenant’s date order — 33 reports

The retired booking-v3 form displayed an MM/DD/YYYY hint to tenants whose locale is day-first. People followed the hint, then the form read the value using the tenant's day-first convention, so month and day were exchanged.

GitHub ticket: hint: booking-v3 shows the wrong date order
